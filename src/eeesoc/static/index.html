Live

Every in-play game, ticking in realtime — green = home, blue = away. Each chiclet lists who scored and who came on, with the minute, and each club’s ⚡ clinical power — goals per 100 xG this season (100 = scores what its chances are worth; above is clinical, below wasteful), 🎯 offence power — everything that leads to goals: chances created, shot volume, shots on target, pressure (corners) and goals per game against the league (above 100 potent, below blunt) with goals scored per game, 🛡 defence power — how few chances it allows against the league average (above 100 solid, below leaky) with goals allowed per game, 📈 momentum — recent form from the last five results, newest weighted heaviest (above 100 rising, below fading), 🥅 last 5 scored / allowed — how many goals each club scored and allowed across those same five games, broken by full-match total and first half, and 🔮 potential — underlying strength from chance quality both ways, with whether results lag it (upside) or run ahead of it (overachieving), and 🧤 clean sheets — the season total, how many in the last five, and the share of games kept clean — each with its rank in the league. 🥅 goals-per-half rows follow the clock: before the break, how often each club’s first halves produced 0 / 1 / 2 / 3+ goals in total, scored and allowed; after it, the same three splits over only the club’s past games that stood at this half-time score from that club’s side — a 2–0 home lead looks up the home club’s games when they led 2–0, and the away club’s games when they trailed 0–2. Markers for shots (incl. blocked), SOT, goals, corners, an xG vs time chart, a rolling 15′ pressure bar for who is in the other side’s final third right now, a pressure graph of that same window at every minute so you can see who was pinning whom 10′ ago, a possession graph and a duel graph — each with three lines (home, away, 50%) over that same last 15′ window at every minute — and a territory heat of where the ball has actually been in those last 15′ (not the whole match). Switch to Finished for the same chiclet on today’s and yesterday’s full-time games, or Upcoming for today’s kickoffs that have not started yet. Tap a chiclet for the pitch graphic — last 15′ of the ball, fading older touches, home attacking right — and each team's formation and XI — every player's match power number, a freshness bar by minutes played, and ⇄ sub markers with the minute and who came off — ▾ to collapse, or hold and drag ⠿ to reorder the board. League chips stack: click EPL then La Liga to see both.

0-0 at half time

Every archived game that went in level at 0-0, broken out by league and cut at 45′ — the same event strip and xG chart as Live, so you can read what a goalless half actually looks like. The profile card gives the 80% band (the middle of the pack, 10th to 90th percentile) for shots, on-target, corners and xG, and what happened after the break. Switch to Similar live to score today’s 0-0 first halves against the archive, minute for minute, and see how the closest lookalikes finished.

No more goals this half

For every live game the dashboard scores P(no goal for the rest of this half) from the archive’s goal hazard by minute (stoppage time included), the league’s scoring rate, whether the game is still 0–0, and the edge found by working backwards from every 0–0 half: passes into the penalty box — how often these two clubs’ halves usually reach the box, and how many entries this half has produced so far. When it clears the threshold inside the betting window it fires once per half — on the chiclet and, with EEESOC_DISCORD_WEBHOOK set, in Discord — then tracks the half to held or busted. The live record is a daily bar (green held, red busted) — click a day to open those game chiclets. The tables below replay the whole archive so you can see what each threshold would have done, and whether the probabilities mean what they say. 1 / P is the decimal price you need to break even.
